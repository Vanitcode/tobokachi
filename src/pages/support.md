## Common questions

### I didn't get today's reminder

Check that notifications are allowed for Tobokachi in **Settings → Notifications → Tobokachi**, and that you have set a study time in the app. The reminder arrives at that time, when the day's sheet is ready.

### My cards don't appear on my other device

Tobokachi syncs through your own iCloud. Both devices need to use the same Apple Account, with iCloud turned on for Tobokachi (**Settings → your name → iCloud**). Open the app on both devices and give it a few minutes.

### How do I print the sheet?

Open the day's sheet in the app, tap the **Share** button and choose **Print**. You can also AirDrop the PDF to a Mac and print it from there.

### How do I send the sheet to my e-ink tablet?

Tap **Share** on the sheet and choose your tablet's app. Most e-ink tablets have an iPhone app that accepts PDFs. Some also let you email documents to the device.

### Which CSV files can I import?

Any CSV file. The first row is read as the column names. Then you choose which column holds the word, which one holds the translation and, if you want, which one holds the deck.

### How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. To remove the copy in your iCloud too, go to **Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage** and delete Tobokachi's data. Tobokachi doesn't store anything anywhere else: see the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
