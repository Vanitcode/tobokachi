# Landing page (all languages)

Scope: `src/components/Home.astro` and its sections, en/es/de/fr/ja. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: language learners who learn by writing by hand, e-ink owners. Action: Download on the App Store.
Proof: the real handwritten sheet (ink PNGs), the real e-ink photo, real FSRS intervals. No invented testimonials or numbers.
Constraints: keep the handwriting animation over the sheet; no bottom fade on the sheet; nothing toyish, techy, cream or cluttered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The sheet is the system. The page is set in the PDF's own materials instead of a costume of them; it refuses the gray-desk editorial look (italic display serif, tracked mono kickers, hairlines) and the phone-mockup app template.
OWN-WORLD: White paper, black ink, Apple system faces exactly as the PDF (ui-serif New York for words and titles, system-ui SF Pro for labels, ui-monospace SF Mono for numbers), 1.5 px black card borders with 6 px corners, dashed answer lines, a 2 px black header rule. Colour comes only from the four rating tints (coral #F0997B, amber #FAC775, lime #C0DD97, teal #5DCAA5) with their faces, used as whole fields, plus the pen inks on the sheet.
STORY: The visitor sees a real sheet being answered by hand, understands "the app prints today's cards, you write, you rate", sees how a rating moves the next review, and downloads.
FIRST VIEWPORT: White page. Masthead set like the PDF header (title centred feel, small labels over bold values, 2 px rule). Left: headline in New York bold, short lead, black App Store button with "Free · No account · No ads". Right: the full sheet at large scale, straight, ending in its own footer, ink animating; a band of the four rating tints sits under the sheet as its rating row.
FORM: grounded "the sheet is the system" (user-steered after two safer re-rolls; derived from the app's SimpleCardPracticePageView). seed:0a16db3c3182a4ea
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
